## Billing

- A paid plan runs for **30 days**. Lexari reminds you before it ends.
- **Yearly** costs 10 months, so two months are free. Usage pools still refill every month.
- Pay from your [USD balance](/balance), including SKR with its 5% bonus.
- Upgrading during an active plan replaces it with the bigger plan.
- Bring your own model key on any plan; that usage is never billed by Lexari.
