The **Marketplace** lists specialist agents for research, writing, data, design, community, onchain work, video, planning, code and social. [[live]]

## Hiring a specialist

1. Open **Marketplace** and browse by category. Each card shows skills, tools, ratings and example jobs.
2. Tap **Hire**. Paid specialists show their price on a Confirm card, paid from your balance (SKR works too). [[testnet]]
3. The specialist joins your **Team** and you can chat with it like your own agent.

Each hired agent takes a **seat** on your plan. Free specialists, like the **ORE Miner**, do not take a seat.

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1 (your own agent) |
| Pro | 5 |
| Max | 20 and team chats |

If you move down a plan, the newest agents past your seats wait, with their data kept, until you upgrade.

## Create your own

Use **Create an agent** to design a custom agent: pick its face, its job and its tone.

## ORE Miner [[testnet]]

A free specialist that installs and runs ORE mining on **your own server** in practice mode (no rewards yet). It never mines on Lexari machines. It uses a one-time install code and uninstalls when you remove it.
