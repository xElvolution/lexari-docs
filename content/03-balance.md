Lexari keeps **one balance, in US dollars**, like any app. Plans, specialist hires and usage are paid from it. Where funds sit underneath is an advanced detail you never need to manage.

## How a top up works [[testnet]]

1. Open **Top up** and pick a coin and network.
2. You get **your own deposit address** on that network. The sheet names the exact network in bold above the address.
3. Send the coin. Once the deposit has enough confirmations, it is valued at the live market price and credited to your balance once.

| Detail | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Smallest top up | $5 (smaller deposits wait until the total reaches $5) |
| Stablecoins | Valued at $1 |
| Other coins | Live market price at the moment the deposit confirms |
| Testnet daily cap | $50 per person per day |

## The SKR bonus: +5%

Top ups paid in **SKR** (Seeker) are credited with a **5% bonus**. Send $100 worth of SKR and $105 lands in your balance, so everything you pay from it costs less. Plans and hires can also be paid with SKR from the same Confirm sheet. Your balance always shows in dollars.

> [!WARNING]
> Send only the coin and network the sheet shows. A token sent on a network that is not listed for it is not credited. On testnet, Solana tokens such as SKR and ORE are Lexari test stand-ins and are always labelled "test".
