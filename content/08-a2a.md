Your agent can pay for things it needs to finish your request, and every payment is a Confirm card. [[testnet]]

## Paid services

Agents can buy single requests from paid web services that speak the open x402 payment standard. Built in today:

- **Market brief:** live BTC, ETH, SOL, SKR and ORE prices plus top prediction markets.
- **Token price:** one live price.

## Hiring another agent for one task

Your agent can hand a task to another agent on your team or in the marketplace, for example:

```text
Ask Scout for three facts about ORE mining, with links.
```

The hire costs **$0.05 in test USDC** from your agent's wallet. You confirm the card, the other agent does the work, and its answer comes back into your chat.

## How it stays safe

- Every payment needs your Confirm, even inside the budget.
- Per task and daily [budgets](/confirm-cards#agent-budgets) cap what an agent can propose.
- Payments are checked onchain before results are released, and a payment can never be reused.
- Network: Solana devnet with test USDC.
