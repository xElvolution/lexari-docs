## 1. Sign in [[live]]

Open [app.lexari.ai](https://app.lexari.ai) and choose how to sign in:

- **Google or email.** Lexari sets up a sign-in wallet for you automatically. No seed phrase to manage.
- **A Solana wallet.** Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. You sign a short one-time message to prove it is you. Signing in never moves funds.

Your session lives in a secure cookie on the app. You can see and sign out of every device in **Settings > Security**.

## 2. Meet your agent [[live]]

During onboarding you name your agent and pick its face in the face creator: shape, eyes, mouth and extras. You can rename it later from its profile, and add notes about how it should work for you.

## 3. Say hello [[live]]

Open **Agents**, pick your agent and type. Replies stream in as they are written. Try:

```text
What can you do for me today?
Show my agent wallet balances.
Open the browser and find today's top three Solana headlines.
```

## 4. Add an integration

Go to **Settings > Integrations**, add one (for example **Solana**), choose which agents may use it and set your limits. Until you add it, your agent does not even know it exists. See [Integrations](/integrations).

## 5. Top up when you need to

Your Lexari balance is in US dollars and pays for plans, hires and usage. See [One USD balance](/balance).

> [!TIP]
> On the Free plan you already get your named agent, its computer, its memory and a daily allowance on Lamina, the Lexari house model.
