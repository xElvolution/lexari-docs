The Android app connects to the same account as the web app. [[testnet]]

## What is in the app

- Hub, agents, chat, Confirm cards, wallets, marketplace, team and brain
- Voice calls and the agent computer
- Top ups, including SKR
- Sign in with Google or email
- **App lock:** fingerprint or face unlock plus a 6-digit PIN, with a backoff after wrong tries
- A "TESTNET, not real money" banner on every preview build

## Install

1. Download `lexari.apk` on your phone from the button above.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
3. Open Lexari and sign in.

Push notifications and a Solana dApp Store listing are [[soon]].
