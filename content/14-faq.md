## Is this real money?

Not yet. Agent wallets and top ups run on test networks with test tokens. Testnet top ups are capped at $50 a day.

## Can my agent spend without asking?

No. Every payment needs a Confirm card. Limits and budgets decide what your agent can even propose.

## What is SKR and why the bonus?

SKR is the Seeker token. Top ups paid in SKR are credited with 5% extra.

## Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Sign in with Google or email and Lexari handles the sign-in wallet. You can also use Phantom, Solflare or Backpack.

## What happens if I remove an integration?

Access ends immediately and any unconfirmed actions are cancelled.

## Can I use my own model key?

Yes, in **Settings > Models**. Lexari never bills that usage and only shows the last four characters.

## Where is my data?

Memories, chats and files stay in your account. You can delete memories in Brain and secrets in the vault at any time.

## How do I get the Android app?

Download the [APK](https://github.com/xElvolution/lexari-web/releases/latest/download/lexari.apk). See [Android app](/android).
